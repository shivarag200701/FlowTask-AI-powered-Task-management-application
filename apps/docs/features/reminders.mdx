Flowtask reminders take the pressure off remembering due times yourself. When you toggle a reminder on a task, Flowtask automatically sends you an email at the exact moment the task is due — so you can focus on your work and trust the app to nudge you at the right moment.

## How reminders work

When a task has a reminder enabled, Flowtask schedules a notification for the task's due time. The moment that time arrives, an email is dispatched to the address associated with your Flowtask account. No mobile push notification or in-app alert is required — the email finds you wherever you are.

## Enabling a reminder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the task creation form or detail panel">
    Create a new task with `C`, or click an existing task's title to open its detail panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date and time">
    Click the **Due date** field and choose a date. Then set a specific **due time** — for example, `9:00 AM`. A timed reminder requires a time; toggling **All day** disables the reminder option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the Reminder switch">
    Flip the **Reminder** toggle to the on position. A confirmation indicator appears next to the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the task">
    Press `Enter` or click away to save. The reminder is now scheduled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Reminders fire at the task's exact due time. Make sure your due time is correct before saving.</Note>

## Email notification

When the reminder fires, you receive an email containing:

- **Task title** — so you know immediately which task is due
- **"Due Now" indicator** — a clear signal that this task needs your attention right now
- **Complete button** — a one-click button in the email that marks the task as complete without requiring you to open the app

## Notification preferences

To manage your email notification settings, navigate to **Settings → Notifications**. From there you can:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Email notifications** | Master toggle to enable or disable all reminder emails |

Turning off email notifications disables all reminder emails globally. Individual reminders remain saved on your tasks and will resume sending if you turn notifications back on.

## Requirements

Before setting a reminder, make sure both of the following are true:

- **A due date is set** — reminders cannot be scheduled without a due date.
- **A specific due time is set** — all-day tasks do not support timed reminders. If **All day** is toggled on, the Reminder option is unavailable.

<Warning>If you update a task's due time after enabling a reminder, disable and re-enable the Reminder toggle to make sure your reminder reflects the new time.</Warning>
