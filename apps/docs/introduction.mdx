Flowtask is a task management platform designed to help individuals and teams capture, organize, and complete work without the friction of traditional to-do tools. Whether you're juggling personal projects or coordinating across a team, Flowtask brings everything together — tasks, deadlines, priorities, and context — into one fast, focused workspace. With smart views that surface what matters right now, drag-and-drop reordering, full-text search, and email reminders, Flowtask keeps you in flow from the moment you log in.

## Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Task Management" icon="list-check">
    Create, edit, and organize tasks with due dates, priorities, subtasks, and drag-and-drop reordering. Stay on top of your work with an interface built for speed and clarity.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Projects" icon="folder-open">
    Group related tasks into projects to keep your work structured. Switch between list and board views to match how your team thinks and operates.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Smart Views" icon="sparkles">
    Jump straight to what needs your attention with built-in views — Today, Upcoming, and Completed — that automatically surface the right tasks at the right time.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Tags & Search" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Label tasks with custom tags for flexible categorization, then use full-text search to find anything instantly across all your projects and tasks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reminders" icon="bell">
    Never miss a deadline. Set email reminders on any task and let Flowtask notify you when it's time to act, so nothing slips through the cracks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Keyboard Shortcuts" icon="keyboard">
    Move at the speed of thought with keyboard shortcuts for creating tasks, navigating views, and managing your workspace — no mouse required.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
